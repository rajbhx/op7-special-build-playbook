## Problem

Engine Settings bottom sheet did not scroll on the phone (1080x2400): HARDWARE TUNING, Start service, Download GGUF, Stats and TOOL INVENTORY sat below the fold and were unreachable, so the buttons looked 'missing' and Download/Stats could not be exercised

## Root cause

ModalBottomSheet body Column had no scroll modifier; the sheet is full-height (skipPartiallyExpanded), so taller-than-screen content was clipped

## Solution

added .verticalScroll(rememberScrollState()) to the sheet Column
