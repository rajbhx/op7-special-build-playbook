## Problem

split-brain RAM ceiling: native rss_over_limit fired at 1500 MiB while the Kotlin watchdog/UI used 1536 MiB (1.5 GB)

## Root cause

MemoryMonitor.cpp hardcoded 1572864000ULL (=1500 MiB) independently of Op7SystemProfile.MEMORY_LIMIT_BYTES (=1610612736 / 1536 MiB)

## Solution

single constexpr kOp7MemoryLimitBytes = 1610612736ULL in MemoryMonitor.hpp used by statsJson; contract pinned by MemoryBudgetTest.nativeCeilingMatchesKotlinSingleSourceOfTruth (SYSTEM-PARAMETERS.json max_total_runtime = 1536 is authoritative)
